Non-Dangerous Goods (NDG) Declaration Format for Air Cargo India

Air Freight Compliance Mandatory Notice

Whenever shipping liquids, chemical powders, herbal products, or cosmetics by air (via DHL, FedEx, IndiGo CarGo, Air India Cargo, etc.), airlines demand an NDG Declaration Letter accompanied by a validated 16-section GHS Safety Data Sheet (SDS) showing 'Not Regulated' under Section 14.

For Indian exporters, air cargo is the fastest shipping route, but it carries the strictest safety audits. Airlines follow IATA (International Air Transport Association) Dangerous Goods Regulations. If you declare a chemical product as non-hazardous without matching technical proof, your consignment will be rejected at the airport cargo terminal.

Standard NDG Declaration Format (Sample Template)

You can use this standard format on your company letterhead, stamped and signed by an authorized signatory:

[ON COMPANY LETTERHEAD] Date: [DD/MM/YYYY] TO WHOMSOEVER IT MAY CONCERN / AIRLINE CARGO ACCEPTANCE DESK Subject: NON-DANGEROUS GOODS DECLARATION FOR AIR CARGO AWB / Booking No.: [Air Waybill Number or Reference] Product Trade Name: [Full Product Name] Chemical / Botanical Identity: [Active Ingredient / Chemical Name] Quantity & Packaging: [e.g., 25 Kg Fiber Drums / HDPE Cans] This is to certify that the consignment mentioned above does not fall under any of the 9 Dangerous Goods classes as defined by current IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) and ICAO Technical Instructions. We hereby declare that the material: 1. Is NOT explosive, flammable, toxic, corrosive, oxidising, or radioactive. 2. Contains NO self-reactive substances or organic peroxides. 3. Flash Point (Closed Cup) is [e.g., > 100°C], which is well above the 60°C threshold for Class 3 flammable liquids. 4. Does NOT emit flammable or toxic gases when in contact with water or air. The cargo is completely safe for transport on passenger and cargo aircraft. Attached herewith is the 16-Section GHS Safety Data Sheet (SDS) prepared in accordance with UN GHS Rev. 10 standards. For [Your Company Name] Authorized Signatory Name: Designation: Contact Number: Company Seal / Stamp:

Why Airlines Reject NDG Declarations

A signed letter alone is rarely enough. Airline DGR screeners cross-examine the letter with your product's Safety Data Sheet:

  • Mismatch in Section 9 Flash Point: If an essential oil or solvent is declared non-hazardous, but Section 9 states a flash point below 60°C, the declaration is rejected immediately as Class 3 Flammable.
  • Blank or Ambiguous Section 14: Stating merely "Safe" instead of "Not Regulated as Dangerous Goods under IATA-DGR / ICAO" leads to shipment holds.
  • No CAS Numbers Disclosed: Customs and airline security officers require verifiable CAS numbers to confirm the chemical is not on restricted precursor lists.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is an NDG certificate required for courier shipments (FedEx/DHL)?

Yes. International couriers like FedEx, DHL Express, and Aramex mandate an NDG letter and compliant SDS for all liquids, creams, powders, and chemicals before booking pickup.

Can ChemSDS verify if our product qualifies as Non-Dangerous Goods?

Yes. We evaluate your formulation, flash point, and toxicology data against UN Model Regulations and IATA classification tables to determine accurate transport status.
