What is a Non-Dangerous Goods (NDG) Declaration?

A Non-Dangerous Goods (NDG) Declaration Letter (also called a Non-Hazardous Certificate) is a legally binding statement issued on the shipper's company letterhead. It certifies that a cargo or parcel consignment does not contain substances classified under IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) or the IMDG Code.

International courier services such as FedEx, DHL Express, Blue Dart, UPS, and Aramex routinely demand an NDG letter along with a matching 16-section Safety Data Sheet (SDS) before accepting liquids, powders, cosmetics, herbal extracts, essential oils, or battery items.

Non-Hazardous Declaration Letter Format for Courier (FedEx, DHL, Blue Dart)

Below is the standard, airline-approved format required by courier customs and safety officers. Print this declaration on your Official Company Letterhead:

TO WHOMSOEVER IT MAY CONCERN Date: [Insert Date] Airway Bill (AWB) / Tracking No: [Insert Courier AWB Number] Invoice No & Date: [Insert Commercial Invoice Number] Destination Country: [Insert Destination Country] DECLARATION OF NON-DANGEROUS / NON-HAZARDOUS GOODS We, [Insert Shipper Company Name], having our registered facility at [Insert Registered Address], hereby declare that the shipment handed over to [FedEx / DHL / Blue Dart / Airline Name] under the aforementioned Airway Bill contains the following product(s): • Product Commercial Name: [Insert Exact Product Name] • Technical / Chemical Name: [Insert Composition or INCI Name] • Total Quantity / Packages: [e.g., 5 Bottles x 1 Liter / 2 Boxes] We certify that the above-mentioned consignment: 1. Is NOT classified as Dangerous Goods under the current edition of the IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) Special Provisions. 2. Does NOT fall under any of the 9 IATA / UN Hazard Classes (Not Explosive, Not Flammable, Not Toxic, Not Corrosive, Not Radioactive, Not Magnetic). 3. Has a Flash Point above 60°C (140°F) and is completely safe for passenger and cargo aircraft transportation. 4. Is non-hazardous, non-toxic, non-infectious, and non-prohibited for domestic and international courier carriage. 5. In the event of packaging leakage, it will not cause injury to cargo handlers, aircraft structure, or other consignments. This declaration is issued in conjunction with Section 14 (Transport Information) of our GHS-compliant 16-Section Safety Data Sheet (SDS) enclosed herewith. Authorized Signatory: Name: [Signatory Name] Designation: [e.g., Export Manager / Quality Head] Company Seal & Stamp: Contact Phone & Email: [Insert Contact Details]

Top Reasons Couriers Hold or Reject NDG Letters

  • No Matching SDS Attached: Courier safety desks (like DHL DG desk or FedEx DG Hotline) will reject an NDG declaration if it is not supported by a valid 16-section GHS Safety Data Sheet.
  • Missing AWB or Invoice Number: The letter must explicitly link to the physical package tracking or Airway Bill (AWB) number.
  • Conflict in Section 14 of SDS: If your SDS Section 14 shows a UN number (like UN 1993 or UN 1169) but your letter claims "Non-Dangerous", the parcel is flagged immediately for misdeclaration.
  • Not on Official Company Letterhead: Plain printouts without company stamp, registration details, and authorized signature are rejected by port customs.
