Why Amazon Flags Products for Hazmat Review

When you convert an ASIN to Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) or sell products containing liquids, sprays, powders, cosmetics, essential oils, or batteries, Amazon’s automated algorithm flags the listing for a Dangerous Goods (Hazmat) Review.

Until cleared by Amazon's compliance team, inventory cannot be checked in, shipment plans are locked, and buy-box sales freeze. Amazon requires either a verified 16-Section GHS Safety Data Sheet (SDS) or a certified Exemption Sheet within 14 business days.

Top 5 Reasons Amazon Rejects Safety Data Sheets

  • 1. Title & Brand Discrepancy: The product name on Section 1 of the SDS does not match the exact title or brand on the Amazon product detail page.
  • 2. Outdated Format / Legacy MSDS: Submitting obsolete 8- or 9-section material safety data sheets instead of current GHS Rev. 10 / OSHA HazCom 2012 format.
  • 3. Missing CAS Numbers or Percentages: Section 3 fails to declare 100% composition or valid Chemical Abstracts Service (CAS) numbers for hazardous ingredients.
  • 4. Conflicting Transport Data: Section 14 (Transport Information) contradicts Amazon’s internal FBA transportation limits (IATA / IMDG).
  • 5. Document Older than 5 Years: Amazon automatically declines SDS files dated more than 5 years prior to the submission date.

Step-by-Step: Uploading to Amazon Seller Central

  1. Log in to Amazon Seller Central.
  2. Navigate to Inventory > Manage Dangerous Goods Classification.
  3. Click on Upload Document on the right-hand panel.
  4. Enter your ASIN, select Safety Data Sheet (SDS), select language, and upload the verified PDF.
  5. The Amazon Dangerous Goods team reviews the document within 2 to 4 business days.
