Whether you are an Indian chemical manufacturer, a merchant trader shipping goods overseas, or an e-commerce seller facing Amazon FBA Hazmat review, having a valid Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS)—now globally standardized as a Safety Data Sheet (SDS)—is mandatory by law.

Is MSDS a "Government Certificate"?

A common misconception among Indian business owners is that an MSDS is an official government-issued license or certificate from a portal like DGFT or Ministry of Chemicals. In reality, an MSDS is a comprehensive technical declaration document.

The law requires the manufacturer or supplier to author and supply an authentic 16-section document that accurately reflects the chemical hazards, workplace exposure limits, and transport rules under the Globally Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals (GHS).

The 4 Steps to Prepare Your MSDS in India

Step 1: Compile Product Composition & Technical Data

You must provide the chemical formulation. For mixtures, this means knowing the percentage breakdown of active ingredients and their CAS (Chemical Abstracts Service) numbers. If you have laboratory test reports (TDS, flash point testing), keep them ready.

Step 2: Collect Business & Export Documentation

For port, customs, and commercial validation, your SDS must display the official corporate details of the Indian entity, including registered address, emergency phone contact, and company verification (GST / IEC details).

Step 3: Author the Mandatory 16 GHS Sections

Standard Indian and international trade rejects obsolete 8-to-9 section formats. Every document must strictly follow the international 16-section GHS architecture:

  • Section 01–03: Chemical Identification, Hazard Classification, Ingredients
  • Section 04–08: First-aid, Firefighting, Spills, Handling, PPE Controls
  • Section 09–13: Physical/Chemical Properties, Stability, Toxicology, Disposal
  • Section 14–16: Transport (UN/IATA/IMDG), Regulatory Law, Revision Notes
Step 4: Verification & Final Delivery

Unless you have an in-house chemical toxicologist, drafting this manually carries substantial liability risks (such as port detentions or shipping penalties). Professional regulatory authoring guarantees compliance with air, sea, and customs regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I generate an MSDS using free online templates?

Free online templates almost always fail customs and airline audits because they contain missing CAS toxicology data, incorrect hazard pictograms, or empty Section 14 transport blocks. If customs or an airline surveyor rejects your sheet, demurrage and container hold charges will vastly exceed the cost of professional authoring.

What is the validity period of an MSDS in India?

Under international standards, an SDS/MSDS should be reviewed and updated every 3 to 5 years, or immediately whenever there is a change in product formulation, new toxicological data, or revisions to national transport laws.

Will this MSDS work for both domestic sales and global exports?

Yes. When authored in standard GHS Rev. 10 format, your sheet can be tailored for domestic Indian trade as well as export destinations including the United States (OSHA HazCom), the European Union (EU REACH / CLP), the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.
