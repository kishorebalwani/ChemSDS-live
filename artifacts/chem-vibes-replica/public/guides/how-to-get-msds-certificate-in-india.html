⚡ How to Obtain an SDS / MSDS in India (Quick Steps)

  1. Gather Product Details: Collect product name, 100% composition breakdown, CAS numbers, physical state, and flash point.
  2. Prepare Required Documents: Keep your Importer-Exporter Code (IEC), GST registration details, and Certificate of Analysis (COA) or lab test results ready.
  3. Engage a Specialized Regulatory Agency: Send your product specifications to an experienced chemical compliance provider (such as ChemSDS) instead of generic documentation vendors.
  4. Review and Issue 16-Section SDS: Regulatory toxicologists calculate classification, assign GHS pictograms, draft Section 14 transport data (IATA/IMDG), and issue the export-ready document.

Is an MSDS Really a "Government Certificate"?

There is a common misconception among Indian exporters that an MSDS is an official certificate issued by a government bureau (like CDSCO, BIS, or DGFT). In international chemical trade, an SDS (Safety Data Sheet) is a technical regulatory disclosure document authored strictly in compliance with United Nations GHS (Globally Harmonized System) standards.

Customs authorities, airlines (IATA), shipping lines (IMDG), and platforms like Amazon FBA require this document to verify safety handling, flammability, and transport hazard classifications before releasing cargo.

Documents Required for SDS / MSDS Authoring in India

  • Product Identification: Commercial trade name and intended industrial/consumer application.
  • Chemical Formulation / Recipe: Complete list of active and inactive ingredients with percentages summing up to 100%.
  • CAS Numbers: Chemical Abstracts Service registry numbers for each classified component.
  • Physical & Chemical Parameters: Flash point, pH value, boiling point, and appearance (liquid, powder, slurry).
  • Export Particulars: IEC code and destination country (to apply specific local rules like US OSHA, EU REACH, or WHMIS).
